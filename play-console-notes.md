# Google Play Reviewer Notes

To review the app without a LibreLinkUp account, tap **"Try Demo Mode"** on the start screen.

Demo Mode:
- uses simulated glucose data for approximately the last 2 months,
- includes multiple demo monitored people,
- does not require login,
- does not call real LibreLinkUp APIs,
- is clearly marked in the UI as demo data.

Medical disclaimer shown in app:
LibreCare is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendations or emergency alerts.
